Privacy Policy for Crew UTC Log

Effective date: September 10, 2026

Crew UTC Log is a flight-crew utility application designed to help users record and manage UTC/flight-related events, sectors, notes, timers, planning information, and related operational information.

This Privacy Policy explains what information the app handles, why it is handled, and how third-party services used by the app may process information.

1. Information stored on your device

Crew UTC Log stores information that you enter or create in the app, such as flight/event log entries, sector information, notes, preferences, timers, saved UTC screenshots, and other app settings. This information is stored locally on your Android device using local application storage/database mechanisms.

The app does not require you to create an account, provide your name, or provide an email address to use its core functionality.

2. Weather and approximate location information

The app includes a weather feature. When weather information is requested, the app contacts ipapi to obtain an approximate location based on the device's internet connection/IP address. The returned city and approximate latitude/longitude are then used to request weather information from Open-Meteo.

The app does not need to store your precise GPS location for this weather feature. Weather requests may nevertheless involve your IP address being processed by the third-party service used for IP-based geolocation. Please review the privacy policies of these third-party services for their own data practices.

3. Voice input

Crew UTC Log includes optional voice-command functionality. If you choose to use voice input, the app uses Android's speech-recognition facilities and requires microphone access for that feature. Audio is used to perform speech recognition. The app does not intentionally store voice recordings as part of its own local database.

Because Android speech recognition may be provided by a system or third-party recognition service, processing of voice data may be subject to the privacy practices of that service.

4. Camera and photos

The current application package contains camera-related Android libraries, but the current code reviewed for this privacy policy does not identify an active camera-upload or remote photo-storage service. If a future version adds camera capture or photo-upload functionality, this policy will be updated before that functionality is used for collection or sharing.

5. Analytics

Crew UTC Log uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand app usage and improve the app. The app sends limited feature-use events, such as when certain features are opened or used. The custom events implemented by the app do not intentionally include the contents of your flight logs, notes, or other user-entered records.

Google Analytics for Firebase may automatically collect certain app, device, and usage information according to Google's analytics documentation. This processing is performed by Google as a third-party service provider.

For more information, see Google Analytics for Firebase and Google's privacy information at Google Privacy & Terms.

6. Information we do not intentionally collect

The app does not intentionally collect or transmit your flight log database, notes, sector records, or saved UTC screenshots to the app developer's own servers.

The app does not contain advertising SDKs in the current version and does not intentionally sell personal information.

7. Third-party services

The current version uses or may use the following third-party services:

  • Google Analytics for Firebase – app usage and analytics.
  • ipapi – approximate IP-based location used to determine a city for weather.
  • Open-Meteo – weather data based on coordinates.
  • Android system speech recognition – optional voice input.

These third parties operate under their own privacy policies and terms. Crew UTC Log does not control how third-party services independently process information after receiving requests from the app.

8. Data retention and deletion

Information stored locally by Crew UTC Log remains on your device until you delete it through the app, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app, subject to normal Android/device backup behavior.

Analytics and third-party service data are retained and handled by the respective service providers according to their own policies and retention practices.

9. Data security

Crew UTC Log is designed to keep user-created operational records in local app storage rather than transmitting those records to the developer's own server. Network communications used by the app's current weather services use HTTPS.

No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and users should keep their Android device and operating system protected.

10. Children's privacy

Crew UTC Log is a general-purpose flight-crew utility and is not directed specifically at children. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

11. Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the app's features, third-party services, or data practices change. The updated version will be made available at the same public URL and will include a revised effective date when appropriate.
